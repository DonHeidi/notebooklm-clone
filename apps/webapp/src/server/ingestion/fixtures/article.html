Early Civilisations

The first human civilisations arose independently in several river valleys. Mesopotamia, between the Tigris and the Euphrates, developed cuneiform writing and city-states such as Uruk and Ur.

Egypt unified along the Nile under pharaonic rule, while the Indus valley supported planned cities like Harappa and Mohenjo-daro with sophisticated drainage systems.
